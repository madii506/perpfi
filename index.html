Fees in. Profit out. Forever.

03

Fees are margin

Every trade of the coin pays creator fees. They flow into the coin's own Hyperliquid account and keep its position funded.

04

Profit buys back

When the position is up, profit is taken and buys the coin back from the market, then burns it. Every fill is public.

coin trades→creator fees→Hyperliquid margin→profit→buyback + burn→coin trades∞
04 — FAQ

Questions.

Is PERPFI part of pump.fun or Hyperliquid?

No. PERPFI is independent. It launches coins on pump.fun and trades on Hyperliquid through their public tools.

Can a coin's position lose?

Yes. Perps can lose and can be liquidated. The liquidation price on the card is an estimate from leverage only. Fees keep feeding margin, but nothing here guarantees a profit.

Where do the fees go?

Into the coin's own Hyperliquid account as margin. Profit taken from the position buys the coin back and burns it.

When can I launch?

Launches open with $PERPFI. The builder above already runs on live Hyperliquid prices, so you can line up your coin now.

Will anyone DM me?

No. Nobody from PERPFI will DM you or ask for a seed phrase.
